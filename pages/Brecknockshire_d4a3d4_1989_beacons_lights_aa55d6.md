## Introduction

The 1989 Brecon Beacons lights case is one of the better-known modern UFO reports associated with historic [Brecknockshire]({{ 'brecknockshire-ufos-lights-claims-and/' | relative_url }}). The incident is remembered because it combines several features that often make night-time sightings difficult to assess: a remote mountain setting, a small number of witnesses, unusual descriptions of coloured lights, and a lack of physical evidence. The available record comes mainly from later witness interviews and UFO investigation reports rather than official documentation. It remains an unidentified sighting in the strict sense that the observers did not identify what they saw; it is not evidence that an extraordinary craft was present.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.bufog.com/post/november-1989-brecon-beacons-multi-lighted-ufo-sighting" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bufog.com">[Birmingham UFO Group]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bufog.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">november 1989 brecon beacons multi lighted ufo sighting</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Birmingham UFO GroupNovember 1989 - Brecon Beacons - Multi-lighted UFO SightingDecember 29, 2008...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: november 1989</span></span></span>

## The family sighting and reported details

### A mountain-road encounter in November 1989

The commonly cited account was recorded decades later by the Birmingham UFO Group after interviews with witnesses Katie Hodrien and her great aunt Janice. According to that account, the pair were travelling with children across the Brecon Beacons while returning from a visit in England to south-west Wales. It was early evening, with clear weather and little cloud, and the witnesses described seeing unusual lights over the surrounding moorland.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.bufog.com/post/november-1989-brecon-beacons-multi-lighted-ufo-sighting" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bufog.com">[Birmingham UFO Group]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bufog.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">november 1989 brecon beacons multi lighted ufo sighting</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Birmingham UFO GroupNovember 1989 - Brecon Beacons - Multi-lighted UFO SightingDecember 29, 2008...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: november 1989</span></span></span>

The first stage of the sighting involved a distant object or group of lights. Katie, who was sitting in the back of the car, reported seeing a row of coloured lights that appeared to flash or change colour, mainly white and red with other colours also noticed. The lights were interpreted by the witnesses as unlike a normal aircraft because of their arrangement and apparent movement.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.bufog.com/post/november-1989-brecon-beacons-multi-lighted-ufo-sighting" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bufog.com">[Birmingham UFO Group]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bufog.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">november 1989 brecon beacons multi lighted ufo sighting</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Birmingham UFO GroupNovember 1989 - Brecon Beacons - Multi-lighted UFO SightingDecember 29, 2008...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: november 1989</span></span></span>

The account then developed into what made the case particularly memorable. The witnesses said the lights appeared to come closer and lower, eventually seeming to form a curved line. They described the object as large, possibly disc-shaped, although the main body was not clearly visible. Janice reportedly stopped the car and attempted to obtain a photograph, but the attempt failed because she could not operate the camera’s night setting and the situation made continuing the attempt impractical.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.bufog.com/post/november-1989-brecon-beacons-multi-lighted-ufo-sighting" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bufog.com">[Birmingham UFO Group]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bufog.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">november 1989 brecon beacons multi lighted ufo sighting</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Birmingham UFO GroupNovember 1989 - Brecon Beacons - Multi-lighted UFO SightingDecember 29, 2008...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: november 1989</span></span></span>

A further detail in the later account was a sudden bluish-white glow that illuminated the hills after the main sighting. Janice associated this glow with the earlier object, although there was no independent observation or recording to establish what produced it.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.bufog.com/post/november-1989-brecon-beacons-multi-lighted-ufo-sighting" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bufog.com">[Birmingham UFO Group]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bufog.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">november 1989 brecon beacons multi lighted ufo sighting</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Birmingham UFO GroupNovember 1989 - Brecon Beacons - Multi-lighted UFO SightingDecember 29, 2008...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: november 1989</span></span></span>

## Investigations, explanations and unanswered questions

The evidence for the 1989 case is almost entirely witness-based. This gives the report value as a record of human observation, but it limits what can be concluded about the cause. UFO investigators often treat witness consistency as one factor when assessing cases, while also recognising that memories of unusual events can change over time, especially when accounts are discussed or revisited many years later.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.bufog.com/post/november-1989-brecon-beacons-multi-lighted-ufo-sighting" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bufog.com">[Birmingham UFO Group]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bufog.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">november 1989 brecon beacons multi lighted ufo sighting</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Birmingham UFO GroupNovember 1989 - Brecon Beacons - Multi-lighted UFO SightingDecember 29, 2008...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: november 1989</span></span></span>

The investigation published by the Birmingham UFO Group argued that the reported combination of silent movement, multiple lights and apparent hovering did not fit an ordinary aircraft explanation. However, this conclusion reflects the interpretation of the investigators and witnesses rather than a confirmed finding.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.bufog.com/post/november-1989-brecon-beacons-multi-lighted-ufo-sighting" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bufog.com">[Birmingham UFO Group]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bufog.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">november 1989 brecon beacons multi lighted ufo sighting</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Birmingham UFO GroupNovember 1989 - Brecon Beacons - Multi-lighted UFO SightingDecember 29, 2008...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: november 1989</span></span></span>

Several conventional possibilities remain relevant:

* **Aircraft or helicopters:** The Brecon Beacons have long had connections with military training activity, meaning aircraft activity is an important consideration in any unusual lights report from the area. A distant aircraft viewed at night can appear unusual because its navigation lights may be visible without the aircraft structure being clear.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.bufog.com/post/november-1989-brecon-beacons-multi-lighted-ufo-sighting" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bufog.com">[Birmingham UFO Group]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bufog.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">november 1989 brecon beacons multi lighted ufo sighting</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Birmingham UFO GroupNovember 1989 - Brecon Beacons - Multi-lighted UFO SightingDecember 29, 2008...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: november 1989</span></span></span>
* **[Military activity]({{ 'military-activity/' | relative_url }}):** The region’s training landscape provides a realistic source of unusual aerial observations. However, no publicly available record identified the 1989 report as a confirmed military aircraft event.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.bufog.com/post/november-1989-brecon-beacons-multi-lighted-ufo-sighting" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bufog.com">[Birmingham UFO Group]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bufog.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">november 1989 brecon beacons multi lighted ufo sighting</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Birmingham UFO GroupNovember 1989 - Brecon Beacons - Multi-lighted UFO SightingDecember 29, 2008...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: november 1989</span></span></span>
* **Weather or atmospheric effects:** The reported hillside illumination could have had natural explanations, although the witness account did not match how Janice interpreted the event. Without measurements or recordings, the cause cannot be established.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.bufog.com/post/november-1989-brecon-beacons-multi-lighted-ufo-sighting" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bufog.com">[Birmingham UFO Group]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bufog.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">november 1989 brecon beacons multi lighted ufo sighting</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Birmingham UFO GroupNovember 1989 - Brecon Beacons - Multi-lighted UFO SightingDecember 29, 2008...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: november 1989</span></span></span>
