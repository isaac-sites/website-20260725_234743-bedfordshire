## Introduction

Official records of UFO reports in [Flintshire]({{ 'what-really-happened-in-flintshires/' | relative_url }}) show a pattern of public reporting rather than a catalogue of confirmed unexplained objects. The main evidence comes from police incident logs, Freedom of Information disclosures, and wider Ministry of Defence (MoD) records that show how unusual aerial observations were recorded and assessed across the UK. In Flintshire, these records are important because they document what people reported seeing, how authorities handled those reports, and why many cases remained unresolved rather than being formally identified.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.northwales.police.uk/cy-GB/SysSiteAssets/foi-media/north-wales/disclosure-2023/2023-570-unidentified-flying-objectunidentified-aerial-phenomena-sightings.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: northwales.police.uk">[North Wales Police]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">northwales.police.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">North Wales Police Uned Rhyddid Gwybodaeth / Freedom of Information Unit</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">North Wales PoliceUned Rhyddid Gwybodaeth / Freedom of Information UnitNovember 28, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 28, 2025</span></span></span>

## Police reports and public records

North Wales Police records provide one of the clearest modern sources for understanding UFO-related reports connected with Flintshire and the surrounding region. Freedom of Information releases show that police systems contain calls and incident entries involving terms such as UFO, unidentified aerial phenomena (UAP), unusual lights, drones and related descriptions. These records are primarily operational documents: they show what members of the public contacted police about, rather than the outcome of a scientific investigation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.northwales.police.uk/cy-GB/SysSiteAssets/foi-media/north-wales/disclosure-2023/2023-570-unidentified-flying-objectunidentified-aerial-phenomena-sightings.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: northwales.police.uk">[North Wales Police]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">northwales.police.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">North Wales Police Uned Rhyddid Gwybodaeth / Freedom of Information Unit</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">North Wales PoliceUned Rhyddid Gwybodaeth / Freedom of Information UnitNovember 28, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 28, 2025</span></span></span>

The entries illustrate the range of reports that can become part of the official record. Some involve people reporting lights or objects they could not identify, while others involve unusual personal claims or concerns that required recording because they were made through police channels. The variety is significant because it demonstrates that the category “UFO report” covers many different situations, from possible aircraft [sightings]({{ 'sightings-2088c5/' | relative_url }}) and drone concerns to observations that remain unclear after the initial call.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.northwales.police.uk/cy-GB/SysSiteAssets/foi-media/north-wales/disclosure-2023/2023-570-unidentified-flying-objectunidentified-aerial-phenomena-sightings.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: northwales.police.uk">[North Wales Police]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">northwales.police.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">North Wales Police Uned Rhyddid Gwybodaeth / Freedom of Information Unit</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">North Wales PoliceUned Rhyddid Gwybodaeth / Freedom of Information UnitNovember 28, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 28, 2025</span></span></span>

Police involvement does not usually mean that officers conducted a dedicated UFO investigation. A report of an unidentified object may be logged because it could relate to aviation safety, public concern, suspicious activity, or a possible emergency. In practice, many such reports end at the recording stage unless there is a separate policing issue requiring action. This is why police files are useful historical evidence but are not proof that an unknown object was physically present in the way described by a witness.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.northwales.police.uk/cy-GB/SysSiteAssets/foi-media/north-wales/disclosure-2025/2025-573-sightings.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: northwales.police.uk">[North Wales Police]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">northwales.police.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">North Wales Police Uned Rhyddid Gwybodaeth / Freedom of Information Unit</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">North Wales PoliceUned Rhyddid Gwybodaeth / Freedom of Information UnitJanuary 14, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 14, 2026</span></span></span>

The records also show a limitation of official data: the absence of a large number of detailed Flintshire-specific files does not necessarily mean that nobody reported unusual sightings. Older reports may have been handled through different systems, while many historic Ministry of Defence files were created at national rather than county level. The surviving archive is therefore a record of official attention, not a complete map of every local observation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/explore-the-collection/explore-by-time-period/postwar/ufo-reports/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nationalarchives.gov.uk">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nationalarchives.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National Archives UFO reports</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesUFO reports - The National Archives...</span></span></span>

## Ministry of Defence UFO files

For decades, the Ministry of Defence was the main UK government body responsible for assessing UFO reports that might have a defence or aviation relevance. Its files, now held by The National Archives, contain reports from across Britain, including witness statements, correspondence, internal assessments and explanations considered by officials. The records show that the MoD’s interest was not based on proving extraterrestrial claims, but on assessing whether reports represented a possible threat, aircraft issue or intelligence concern.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/explore-the-collection/explore-by-time-period/postwar/ufo-reports/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nationalarchives.gov.uk">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nationalarchives.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National Archives UFO reports</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesUFO reports - The National Archives...</span></span></span>

The national archive material provides important context for Flintshire because the county’s geography includes aviation-related features, including the former RAF presence at Sealand and busy air routes across North Wales. In areas with military, civilian and training aviation activity, officials often considered conventional explanations such as aircraft, weather balloons, satellites, lighting effects and other known phenomena before leaving a report unresolved.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/explore-the-collection/explore-by-time-period/postwar/ufo-reports/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nationalarchives.gov.uk">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nationalarchives.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National Archives UFO reports</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesUFO reports - The National Archives...</span></span></span>

The MoD published annual UFO reports covering the period from 1997 to 2009, recording dates, locations and brief descriptions of reported sightings. These reports demonstrate that official handling was focused on documentation and assessment rather than a search for evidence of alien activity. The files include many cases that were later explained, while others remained unidentified because the available information was insufficient.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ufo-reports-in-the-uk" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: GOV.UK">[GOV.UK]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">GOV.UK</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UF O reports in the UK</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO reports in the UK - GOV.UK...</span></span></span>

The end of the MoD’s dedicated UFO investigation process is also relevant to interpreting Flintshire cases. The department stopped routinely investigating UFO reports in 2009 after concluding that none of the reports it had received indicated a defence threat requiring continued resources. ## Why some cases remain unidentified

An official “unidentified” label often reflects a lack of information rather than evidence of something extraordinary. A witness may provide a sincere account of seeing a light or object, but investigators need details such as distance, size, speed, direction, weather conditions, radar information and independent observations before an identification can be made. Many historical UFO reports lacked enough reliable data to reach a final conclusion.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/explore-the-collection/explore-by-time-period/postwar/ufo-reports/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nationalarchives.gov.uk">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nationalarchives.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National Archives UFO reports</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesUFO reports - The National Archives...</span></span></span>

Flintshire’s location creates several reasons why uncertainty can occur. The county contains coastal viewpoints, urban lighting, industrial areas and air traffic corridors, all of which can make ordinary objects appear unusual under certain conditions. A bright aircraft seen from an unusual angle, a satellite, a meteor, a drone or atmospheric effects can all produce observations that are difficult to identify after the event.

The difference between “unexplained” and “unexplained by the available evidence” is central to reading official UFO records. A police log or government file may preserve a report because officials could not determine exactly what was seen, but that does not automatically support the conclusion that the object represented unknown technology. The strongest conclusion that can usually be drawn is that a person reported an observation which remained unresolved.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/explore-the-collection/explore-by-time-period/postwar/ufo-reports/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nationalarchives.gov.uk">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nationalarchives.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National Archives UFO reports</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesUFO reports - The National Archives...</span></span></span>

For Flintshire, the official record therefore has value mainly as a historical and social record. It shows how residents interacted with authorities, how institutions categorised unusual aerial reports, and how investigations balanced public concern with the need for evidence. The surviving files support a picture of recurring curiosity and occasional uncertainty, rather than a documented series of confirmed extraordinary events.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.northwales.police.uk/cy-GB/SysSiteAssets/foi-media/north-wales/disclosure-2023/2023-570-unidentified-flying-objectunidentified-aerial-phenomena-sightings.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: northwales.police.uk">[North Wales Police]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">northwales.police.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">North Wales Police Uned Rhyddid Gwybodaeth / Freedom of Information Unit</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">North Wales PoliceUned Rhyddid Gwybodaeth / Freedom of Information UnitNovember 28, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 28, 2025</span></span></span>

    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
