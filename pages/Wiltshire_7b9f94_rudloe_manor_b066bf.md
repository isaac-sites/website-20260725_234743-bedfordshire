## Introduction

RAF Rudloe Manor in [Wiltshire]({{ 'wiltshire-ufo-stories-claims-cases-and/' | relative_url }}) became one of the most discussed locations in British UFO history because it combined two realities: it was a genuine military intelligence site, and it was connected to the official handling of UFO reports. The strongest evidence shows that Rudloe Manor was involved in receiving and processing reports of unidentified aerial sightings through RAF and Ministry of Defence channels. However, the more dramatic claims — that it stored crashed spacecraft, alien bodies, or hidden extraterrestrial technology — remain unsupported by released records.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/help-with-your-research/research-guides/ufos/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nationalarchives.gov.uk">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nationalarchives.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National Archives UFOs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesUFOs - The National Archives...</span></span></span>

The site matters because it sits at the point where documented government activity and public suspicion overlapped. Rudloe Manor’s secrecy came primarily from its military role, including intelligence, communications and security functions, rather than from proven evidence of a secret UFO research programme. ## The site's military role

Rudloe Manor, near Corsham and Box in Wiltshire, had a long defence history before becoming associated with UFO stories. During the Second World War it was used by the RAF as part of No. 10 Group Fighter Command, helping coordinate air defence operations in western England and Wales. After the war, the site became associated with RAF Provost and Security Services and communications units, giving it a reputation as a place connected with sensitive military work.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://rudloemanor.com/history/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: rudloemanor.com">[rudloemanor.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">rudloemanor.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Rudloe Manor...</span></span></span>

This background explains why rumours developed around the site. A secure RAF installation handling intelligence naturally attracted speculation, particularly during the Cold War when many military activities were classified. Rudloe Manor was also linked with underground defence facilities in the wider Corsham area, adding to its image as a hidden government location. The existence of protected military infrastructure was real, but secrecy around defence planning was not in itself evidence of UFO-related activity.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://rudloemanor.com/history/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: rudloemanor.com">[rudloemanor.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">rudloemanor.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Rudloe Manor...</span></span></span>

The location’s association with UFOs grew because it was connected to the military reporting chain. A place responsible for handling unusual aerial reports could easily become interpreted by the public as a place where UFOs were being “studied” in a much broader sense than the official records show.

## UFO reporting procedures and the Rudloe connection

The Ministry of Defence began formally collecting UFO reports during the post-war period. These reports were not treated as proof of extraterrestrial craft; in official usage, a UFO meant an object or event that had not been identified at the time. The National Archives notes that surviving files mainly contain sighting reports, correspondence, policy discussions and parliamentary material rather than evidence of extraterrestrial investigations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/help-with-your-research/research-guides/ufos/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nationalarchives.gov.uk">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nationalarchives.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National Archives UFOs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesUFOs - The National Archives...</span></span></span>

Rudloe Manor became associated with the RAF’s Flying Complaints Flight, which dealt with reports from members of the public and military personnel. The unit’s role was administrative and investigative: gathering information, assessing whether sightings might have a defence relevance, and passing material through official channels. Declassified material confirmed that the RAF site had a role in the UFO reporting process, which helped explain why researchers had long focused on it.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.thesun.co.uk/news/37650001/inside-uk-britain-area-51-aliens-ufo/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: thesun.co.uk">[The Sun]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">thesun.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Sun Alien &#x27;corpses&#x27;, RAF cover-ups &amp; royal nuclear bunkers</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">inside eerie top-secret UFO research base dubbed &#x27;UK&#x27;s Area 51&#x27;December 29, 2025 — A once-classified RAF base, Rudloe Manor in Wiltshire...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 29, 2025</span></span></span>

The existence of a reporting system created an important distinction:

* **A report being investigated did not mean the object was considered alien.**
* **A report being kept confidential did not automatically mean a cover-up of extraordinary evidence.**
* **A military interest in unusual sightings often reflected normal security concerns, such as unidentified aircraft, surveillance risks or possible aviation hazards.**

</div>

The National Archives’ released UFO collections show many cases were eventually linked to ordinary explanations such as aircraft, satellites, weather effects or astronomical objects, although some reports remained unresolved because the available information was incomplete.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/explore-the-collection/explore-by-time-period/postwar/ufo-reports/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nationalarchives.gov.uk">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nationalarchives.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National Archives UFO reports</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesUFO reports - The National Archives...</span></span></span>

## Myth versus documented history

### What the records support

The documented history supports three main points. First, Rudloe Manor was a genuine military site with security responsibilities. Second, UFO reports were handled through official defence structures that involved RAF and Ministry of Defence personnel. Third, some UFO-related material remained classified or was released only later, partly because it passed through normal government security procedures.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://rudloemanor.com/history/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: rudloemanor.com">[rudloemanor.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">rudloemanor.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Rudloe Manor...</span></span></span>

These facts explain why Rudloe Manor became important in UFO culture. Researchers were not inventing the military connection: there really was a link between the site and UFO reporting. The dispute concerns what that connection meant.

### What the records do not show

    var joiner = url.indexOf('?') >= 0 ? '&' : '?';
    var params = [];
    if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
