## Introduction

The Nottingham UFO Investigation Society (NUFOIS) was one of the most important local organisations in the history of UFO reporting in [Nottinghamshire]({{ 'what-really-happened-in-nottinghamshire/' | relative_url }}). Rather than being remembered for one definitive sighting, its significance lies in the way it created a local system for collecting witness accounts, publishing investigations and preserving material from a period when UFO groups were active across Britain. The society helped turn scattered reports from Nottingham and the surrounding Midlands into a recorded archive of public experiences, investigations and debates.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://leftlion.co.uk/features/2019/03/ufo-sightings-nottingham/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: leftlion.co.uk">[LeftLion]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">leftlion.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Left Lion Area NG1: UFO Sightings in Nottingham</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Area NG1: UFO Sightings in Nottingham - Nottingham CultureMarch 8, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 8, 2019</span></span></span>

The surviving record does not demonstrate that Nottinghamshire produced a confirmed extraordinary UFO event. ## The society and its 1970s origins

### How a local group became part of British UFO research

The Nottingham UFO Investigation Society emerged during the growth of organised UFO research in Britain during the 1960s and 1970s. Local groups developed across the country as volunteers attempted to document sightings independently, often using witness interviews, questionnaires, newspaper reports and case files. Nottingham was connected to this wider network through organisations such as the British UFO Research Association (BUFORA), which encouraged structured investigation and the collection of evidence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.bufora.org.uk/about" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bufora.org.uk">[BUFORA]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bufora.org.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">BUFORAABOUT &#124; BUFORA</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">BUFORAABOUT &#124; BUFORA</span></span></span>

NUFOIS became associated with Robert W. Morrell, a Nottingham-based writer, editor and investigator who played a leading role in the group’s activities. Contemporary local coverage describes the organisation as being chaired by Morrell and operating from Nottingham, with a focus on collecting and assessing reports from the city and wider Midlands area.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://leftlion.co.uk/features/2019/03/ufo-sightings-nottingham/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: leftlion.co.uk">[LeftLion]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">leftlion.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Left Lion Area NG1: UFO Sightings in Nottingham</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Area NG1: UFO Sightings in Nottingham - Nottingham CultureMarch 8, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 8, 2019</span></span></span>

The society’s importance came from organisation rather than from proving any particular explanation. UFO reports are often brief observations: unusual lights, objects seen at a distance or events where the witness cannot identify what was observed. Groups such as NUFOIS attempted to preserve these accounts so they could be compared, investigated and discussed rather than disappearing as isolated stories.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://leftlion.co.uk/features/2019/03/ufo-sightings-nottingham/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: leftlion.co.uk">[LeftLion]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">leftlion.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Left Lion Area NG1: UFO Sightings in Nottingham</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Area NG1: UFO Sightings in Nottingham - Nottingham CultureMarch 8, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 8, 2019</span></span></span>

### A regional network rather than a single Nottingham story

Although based in Nottingham, the society’s activities reflected a broader Midlands interest in UFO reports. Historical references indicate that NUFOIS investigated reports not only from Nottingham itself but also from surrounding areas, including parts of neighbouring counties. This wider collecting role helps explain why the society became part of the local UFO record rather than simply a city-based enthusiasts’ club.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.thesparrowsnest.org.uk/collections/public_archive/20212.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: thesparrowsnest.org.uk">[thesparrowsnest.org.uk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">thesparrowsnest.org.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ut OS ZE</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">SNEINTONU BENDIGO &#x27;A No:22 LA VOCEJanuary 8, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 8, 2024</span></span></span>

The geography was also significant. Nottinghamshire contained a mixture of urban areas, transport routes and aviation activity, meaning witnesses could encounter many types of unusual-looking objects, from aircraft and lights seen at unfamiliar angles to genuinely unidentified observations. A local investigation group provided a place where those reports could be recorded and compared.

## Witness reports and published investigations

### *UFOs over Nottingham* and the paper trail of local investigations

One of NUFOIS’s clearest surviving markers is the 1977 publication *UFO’s over Nottingham*. Catalogued by the Open British National Bibliography, the booklet was published by the Nottingham Unidentified Flying Object Investigation Society and credited to R. W. Morrell and the society.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://obnb.uk/p10538415-ufos-over-nottingham" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: obnb.uk">[OBNB]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">obnb.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">p10538415 ufos over nottingham</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO&#x27;s over Nottingham:: Nottingham Unidentified Flying Object Investigation Society 1977:: OBNB, the Open British National Bibliogr...</span></span></span>

The publication represents an important stage in local UFO research: moving from informal conversations and newspaper stories towards a written record. Such publications usually served several purposes at once. They preserved witness narratives, documented cases that investigators considered noteworthy and provided a record for other researchers to examine.

Local reporting on Nottingham’s UFO history also notes that newsletters and documents connected with UFO investigation groups recorded hundreds of reported sightings during the 1970s and early 1980s. These records included ordinary “lights in the sky” reports as well as more unusual claims, showing the wide range of experiences that entered UFO archives.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://leftlion.co.uk/features/2019/03/ufo-sightings-nottingham/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: leftlion.co.uk">[LeftLion]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">leftlion.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Left Lion Area NG1: UFO Sightings in Nottingham</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Area NG1: UFO Sightings in Nottingham - Nottingham CultureMarch 8, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 8, 2019</span></span></span>

### Investigation did not always mean belief

A significant part of NUFOIS’s legacy is that investigation and belief were not necessarily the same thing. Local accounts describe Morrell as a more questioning figure within the UFO community, with reports of disagreements over how strongly some researchers promoted paranormal interpretations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://leftlion.co.uk/features/2019/03/ufo-sightings-nottingham/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: leftlion.co.uk">[LeftLion]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">leftlion.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Left Lion Area NG1: UFO Sightings in Nottingham</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Area NG1: UFO Sightings in Nottingham - Nottingham CultureMarch 8, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 8, 2019</span></span></span>

This tension reflects a wider issue in UFO research. A report can be valuable as a record of what a person experienced without proving that the interpretation of the event is correct. A witness may honestly describe a strange sight, while later investigation may suggest explanations such as aircraft, astronomical objects, balloons or other conventional causes.

For historians, this makes NUFOIS material useful beyond the question of whether any particular sighting was “real”. The archive records how people in Nottinghamshire understood unusual events, how investigators assessed evidence and how UFO culture developed locally.

## Archives, influence and historical value

### Why the archive matters


<div class="content-callout content-callout--key content-callout--takeaway" markdown="1">

The lasting contribution of NUFOIS is its preservation of local UFO history. Many sightings are difficult to study decades later because memories fade, newspapers are incomplete and informal reports disappear. Collections created by investigation groups provide a snapshot of what people reported at the time and how those reports were handled.

</div>

Local coverage has highlighted that Nottingham’s UFO documents became part of an archive of UFO-related material preserved through local collections, giving researchers a record of reports, publications and investigative activity.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://leftlion.co.uk/features/2019/03/ufo-sightings-nottingham/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: leftlion.co.uk">[LeftLion]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">leftlion.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Left Lion Area NG1: UFO Sightings in Nottingham</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Area NG1: UFO Sightings in Nottingham - Nottingham CultureMarch 8, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 8, 2019</span></span></span>

The archive value is especially important because UFO history is not only about unexplained objects. It is also about social history: how communities responded to uncertainty, how amateur researchers organised themselves and how local newspapers and public interest shaped which stories survived.

### From paper files to modern UFO records

NUFOIS operated in a period when investigation depended heavily on personal correspondence, printed newsletters and physical archives. Modern UFO reporting has changed significantly, with online forms, databases, satellite tracking and easier access to aviation information changing how unusual sightings are assessed.

The older Nottingham material therefore provides a historical comparison point. It shows an earlier model of investigation based on collecting testimony and building local knowledge networks. Modern organisations such as BUFORA continue some similar functions through case investigation, research and structured reporting methods, although the wider environment is now much more digitally connected.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.bufora.org.uk/investigations" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bufora.org.uk">[BUFORA]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bufora.org.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">BUFORAINVESTIGATIONS &#124; BUFORA</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">BUFORAINVESTIGATIONS &#124; BUFORA</span></span></span>

### What remains uncertain about the legacy

The main limitation of the NUFOIS record is not a lack of historical importance but the difficulty of verifying many individual reports. Surviving publications and archives can show that a sighting was reported and investigated, but they do not automatically establish what caused the observation.

This distinction is central to understanding Nottinghamshire’s UFO history. NUFOIS’s strongest legacy is as a record-keeping and investigation organisation. It helped preserve local testimony, encouraged a more systematic approach to reports and connected Nottinghamshire to the wider British UFO research movement, while leaving many individual cases open to different interpretations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://leftlion.co.uk/features/2019/03/ufo-sightings-nottingham/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: leftlion.co.uk">[LeftLion]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">leftlion.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Left Lion Area NG1: UFO Sightings in Nottingham</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Area NG1: UFO Sightings in Nottingham - Nottingham CultureMarch 8, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 8, 2019</span></span></span>

    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
