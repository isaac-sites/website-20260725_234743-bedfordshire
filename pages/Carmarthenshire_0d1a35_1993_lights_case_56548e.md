## Introduction

## The reported lights and locations

The 1993 episode involved reports of strange lights seen in Carmarthenshire and the East Dinefwr area, with the story appearing in the *Carmarthen Journal* in September 1993. The newspaper headline described the “UFO riddle” as potentially solved, indicating that the main development was not a new unexplained discovery but an attempt to identify what observers had seen.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://britishnewspaperarchive.co.uk/search/results/1990-01-01/1999-12-31?basicsearch=ufo&amp;mostspecificlocation=carmarthen%2C+carmarthenshire%2C+wales&amp;place=carmarthen%2C+carmarthenshire%2C+wales&amp;retrievecountrycounts=false&amp;somesearch=ufo" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: britishnewspaperarchive.co.uk">[British Newspaper Archive]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">britishnewspaperarchive.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">British Newspaper ArchiveResults for &#x27;ufo&#x27; &#124; Between 1st Jan 1990 and 31st Dec 1999 &#124; Carmarthen, Carmarthenshire, Wales &#124; Location &#124; Bri...</span></span></span>

The details available from surviving archive records are limited. Unlike some British UFO cases, there is no widely available Ministry of Defence file, radar record, photograph collection, or detailed witness transcript that establishes exactly what each observer saw. The case therefore rests mainly on press reporting and the interpretation given at the time.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://britishnewspaperarchive.co.uk/search/results/1990-01-01/1999-12-31?basicsearch=ufo&amp;mostspecificlocation=carmarthen%2C+carmarthenshire%2C+wales&amp;place=carmarthen%2C+carmarthenshire%2C+wales&amp;retrievecountrycounts=false&amp;somesearch=ufo" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: britishnewspaperarchive.co.uk">[British Newspaper Archive]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">britishnewspaperarchive.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">British Newspaper ArchiveResults for &#x27;ufo&#x27; &#124; Between 1st Jan 1990 and 31st Dec 1999 &#124; Carmarthen, Carmarthenshire, Wales &#124; Location &#124; Bri...</span></span></span>

This makes the wording of the case important. A “UFO” in its original sense simply means an unidentified flying object — something observed in the sky that has not yet been identified. It does not automatically mean a spacecraft or an extraordinary explanation. Many reports later turn out to involve ordinary sources such as aircraft, astronomical objects, satellites, weather effects, or distant lights viewed under unusual conditions. The National Archives has highlighted this wider problem in British UFO records, noting that people can report objects they do not recognise when looking at the sky.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://media.nationalarchives.gov.uk/index.php/cold-war-ufos/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: media.nationalarchives.gov.uk">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">media.nationalarchives.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National Archives The Cold War and UFOs &#124; The National Archives</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesThe Cold War and UFOs &#124; The National ArchivesMay 23, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 23, 2019</span></span></span>

## Newspaper coverage and investigation

The main surviving account comes from local newspaper archives. The *Carmarthen Journal* coverage places the event within a period when UFO reports were regularly discussed in local media, with newspapers often reporting both the original claims and attempts to explain them.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://britishnewspaperarchive.co.uk/search/results/1990-01-01/1999-12-31?basicsearch=ufo&amp;mostspecificlocation=carmarthen%2C+carmarthenshire%2C+wales&amp;place=carmarthen%2C+carmarthenshire%2C+wales&amp;retrievecountrycounts=false&amp;somesearch=ufo" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: britishnewspaperarchive.co.uk">[British Newspaper Archive]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">britishnewspaperarchive.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">British Newspaper ArchiveResults for &#x27;ufo&#x27; &#124; Between 1st Jan 1990 and 31st Dec 1999 &#124; Carmarthen, Carmarthenshire, Wales &#124; Location &#124; Bri...</span></span></span>

The investigation process appears to have followed a familiar route: gather reports, compare observations, and look for a known source. The significance of the case lies partly in this transition. The lights were initially treated as a mystery because observers lacked an immediate explanation, but later reporting suggested that a more ordinary cause had been identified.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://britishnewspaperarchive.co.uk/search/results/1990-01-01/1999-12-31?basicsearch=ufo&amp;mostspecificlocation=carmarthen%2C+carmarthenshire%2C+wales&amp;place=carmarthen%2C+carmarthenshire%2C+wales&amp;retrievecountrycounts=false&amp;somesearch=ufo" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: britishnewspaperarchive.co.uk">[British Newspaper Archive]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">britishnewspaperarchive.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">British Newspaper ArchiveResults for &#x27;ufo&#x27; &#124; Between 1st Jan 1990 and 31st Dec 1999 &#124; Carmarthen, Carmarthenshire, Wales &#124; Location &#124; Bri...</span></span></span>

The available archive record does not provide enough detail to reconstruct every step of the investigation or confirm the exact mechanism with certainty. This is an important limitation. A newspaper statement that a mystery has been solved is evidence that an explanation was proposed, but it is not the same as a publicly available technical investigation proving the cause beyond doubt.
