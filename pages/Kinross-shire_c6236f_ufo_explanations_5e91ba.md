## Introduction

[Kinross-shire]({{ 'what-really-happened-in-kinross-shire/' | relative_url }})’s most discussed UFO-related claims are best understood not as proof of unknown aircraft, but as examples of how unusual observations can remain open to competing interpretations. The strongest regional mystery is the 1990 Calvine photograph case, from the wider Perthshire area associated with the historic Kinross-shire region’s surrounding landscape. A sceptical reading does not require assuming that witnesses invented what they saw; it asks whether ordinary explanations, incomplete information, or weaknesses in the evidence can account for the report.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.skeptic.org.uk/2022/09/the-calvine-ufo-photo-skeptics-and-believers-seek-to-explain-the-30-year-old-images/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: skeptic.org.uk">[The Skeptic]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">skeptic.org.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The SkepticThe Calvine UFO photo: skeptics and believers seek to explain the 30 year old images - The SkepticSeptember 30, 2022...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 30, 2022</span></span></span>

## Common alternative explanations

Sceptical explanations of Kinross-shire UFO claims generally focus on misidentification rather than deliberate deception. A witness may accurately describe an unusual sight while still misunderstanding distance, scale, lighting, background scenery, or the identity of an object. This distinction is important: an unexplained report is not automatically evidence of an unexplained technology.

### Aircraft, terrain and atmospheric effects

The Calvine photograph has attracted several conventional interpretations. One possibility is that the image involved an ordinary aircraft viewed in unusual circumstances, combined with confusion about the surrounding landscape. The smaller object in the image has been identified by investigators as resembling a Harrier jet, although the larger object remains disputed. The presence of a recognisable military aircraft creates a simpler explanation for at least part of the sighting: witnesses may have encountered an unusual aviation event rather than a non-human craft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.skeptic.org.uk/2022/09/the-calvine-ufo-photo-skeptics-and-believers-seek-to-explain-the-30-year-old-images/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: skeptic.org.uk">[The Skeptic]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">skeptic.org.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The SkepticThe Calvine UFO photo: skeptics and believers seek to explain the 30 year old images - The SkepticSeptember 30, 2022...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 30, 2022</span></span></span>

Some sceptical analyses have proposed that the apparent object could be connected to landscape features, reflections, or photographic effects rather than a solid airborne vehicle. Other suggestions have included a hoax involving a constructed object photographed at a distance. None of these explanations has achieved universal agreement, but they demonstrate that a striking image can support multiple interpretations when important information—such as the full sequence of photographs, original negatives, and precise viewing conditions—is unavailable.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.skeptic.org.uk/2022/09/the-calvine-ufo-photo-skeptics-and-believers-seek-to-explain-the-30-year-old-images/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: skeptic.org.uk">[The Skeptic]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">skeptic.org.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The SkepticThe Calvine UFO photo: skeptics and believers seek to explain the 30 year old images - The SkepticSeptember 30, 2022...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 30, 2022</span></span></span>

### Secret aircraft and the problem of “unknown” technology

Another explanation often discussed around Calvine is that the object may have been a classified aircraft. The late Cold War period produced strong public interest in secret aviation projects, and the combination of a strange shape and a military jet encouraged speculation about experimental technology. However, the absence of an identification does not itself prove that a secret aircraft was involved. A classified explanation remains one possibility among several, not a confirmed solution.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.skeptic.org.uk/2022/09/the-calvine-ufo-photo-skeptics-and-believers-seek-to-explain-the-30-year-old-images/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: skeptic.org.uk">[The Skeptic]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">skeptic.org.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The SkepticThe Calvine UFO photo: skeptics and believers seek to explain the 30 year old images - The SkepticSeptember 30, 2022...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 30, 2022</span></span></span>

This illustrates a common problem in UFO investigations: replacing one unknown with another. Saying “it was probably a secret aircraft” may be more plausible than an extraterrestrial explanation, but it still requires evidence. Without flight records, technical details, or additional documentation, the claim remains speculative.

## Limits of photographic evidence

Photographs are often treated as stronger evidence than eyewitness accounts, but UFO images can be difficult to interpret without supporting information. A photograph records light reaching a camera sensor; it does not automatically reveal an object’s identity, distance, size, or speed.

The Calvine image demonstrates these limitations clearly. Although the surviving photograph appears unusual, researchers have lacked the complete original photographic set and the wider context needed for a full reconstruction. The missing images, unknown camera details, and unresolved questions about the exact location and circumstances reduce the certainty that can be drawn from the picture alone.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.skeptic.org.uk/2022/09/the-calvine-ufo-photo-skeptics-and-believers-seek-to-explain-the-30-year-old-images/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: skeptic.org.uk">[The Skeptic]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">skeptic.org.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The SkepticThe Calvine UFO photo: skeptics and believers seek to explain the 30 year old images - The SkepticSeptember 30, 2022...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 30, 2022</span></span></span>

Photographic ambiguity is especially important in rural landscapes such as those around Kinross-shire and neighbouring Highland areas. A distant object can appear large because there are few reliable reference points. Mountains, clouds, haze, and perspective can change how observers judge size and movement. A viewer may sincerely report seeing a large craft, while later analysis may find that the apparent scale was misleading.

The [Calvine case]({{ 'calvine-case/' | relative_url }}) also shows why photographs rarely settle UFO disputes by themselves. Supporters may argue that the image captures something unusual, while sceptics may argue that unusual appearance is not the same as unusual origin. Both positions depend on how much weight is given to missing information.

## Why some cases remain unresolved

Some Kinross-shire-related UFO claims remain unresolved because the evidence is incomplete rather than because competing explanations are equally supported. In the Calvine case, the absence of a publicly available full photographic record and the lack of a definitive identification have allowed multiple interpretations to continue.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.skeptic.org.uk/2022/09/the-calvine-ufo-photo-skeptics-and-believers-seek-to-explain-the-30-year-old-images/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: skeptic.org.uk">[The Skeptic]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">skeptic.org.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The SkepticThe Calvine UFO photo: skeptics and believers seek to explain the 30 year old images - The SkepticSeptember 30, 2022...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 30, 2022</span></span></span>

An unresolved case occupies a middle ground. It is different from a proven extraordinary event, but it is also different from a case where a conventional explanation has been firmly demonstrated. Good investigation separates these categories:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* **Explained cases** have enough evidence for a likely identification, such as aircraft, astronomical objects, weather effects, or ordinary objects seen under unusual conditions.
* **Weakly supported cases** rely mainly on memory, incomplete descriptions, or second-hand accounts.
* **Unresolved cases** contain unusual features but lack enough information for a final conclusion.

</div>

Calvine sits mainly in the third category. The report has features that make it historically important, but the remaining uncertainty does not establish that the object was extraordinary. The case is valuable because it shows how evidence is tested: not by asking whether a story is interesting, but whether the available information rules out simpler explanations.

## What sceptical analysis changes about Kinross-shire UFO history

A sceptical approach does not remove the importance of UFO reports in Kinross-shire. Instead, it changes the question being asked.     var url = template.replace('{query}', encodeQuery(query || '')).replace('{query_plain}', String(query || ''));
    var joiner = url.indexOf('?') >= 0 ? '&' : '?';
    var params = [];
    if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
