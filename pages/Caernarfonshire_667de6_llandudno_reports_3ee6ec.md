## Introduction

## Great Orme sightings and local attention

The Great Orme has become the main geographical focus of Llandudno UFO stories. The headland’s height, open views over the Irish Sea and position above the town make it an obvious place for people to watch the sky. It rises prominently above Llandudno and provides wide coastal views, factors that can make distant aircraft, ships, bright planets and atmospheric effects appear unusual to observers.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.greatorme.co.uk/home" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: greatorme.co.uk">[greatorme.co.uk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">greatorme.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Great Orme Exploration Society</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Great Orme Exploration Society</span></span></span>

Local UFO accounts often highlight reports from the late 20th century, particularly stories collected by enthusiasts and repeated in regional UFO discussions. One frequently cited account concerns a 1967 report by fisherman John Davies, who was said to have observed bright lights over the Great Orme while at sea. Another commonly repeated case involves a 1974 report from walkers who described a large illuminated object near the headland. These accounts form part of the area’s UFO folklore, but the surviving public evidence is largely testimonial rather than supported by radar data, photographs with verified provenance, or official confirmation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.llandudno.com/great-orme-ufo-hotspot/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: llandudno.com">[Llandudno]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">llandudno.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Great Orme UFO Hotspot</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Great Orme UFO Hotspot - Llandudno.comJuly 6, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 6, 2024</span></span></span>

## Witness descriptions and uncertainty

Witness reports from Llandudno generally follow a familiar pattern found in UFO cases elsewhere in Britain: observers describe something unusual, but the available information is rarely enough to determine exactly what happened. Reports may include details such as unusual brightness, apparent hovering, sudden movement or unfamiliar shapes. These features can be memorable to witnesses, but they are also affected by viewing conditions, distance, perspective and the limits of human perception at night.

The strongest part of these accounts is often the consistency of a witness description: several people seeing the same light or object, or a report recorded soon after the event. However, consistency alone does not identify an object’s nature. Multiple people can misinterpret the same conventional event, particularly when observing a distant light without reference points for size, speed or altitude.

The absence of stronger evidence is an important part of assessing the Llandudno reports. There is no publicly available record showing that the Great Orme cases produced recovered material, confirmed instrument readings or an official finding of an extraordinary cause. This places them in the category of unresolved local reports rather than confirmed mysteries. The same distinction appears in wider UK UFO records, where official files contain many unusual observations but frequently lack evidence that points beyond ordinary explanations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/explore-the-collection/explore-by-time-period/postwar/ufo-reports/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nationalarchives.gov.uk">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nationalarchives.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National Archives UFO reports</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesUFO reports - The National Archives...</span></span></span>

## Possible explanations for unusual objects

Many Llandudno reports can be considered alongside ordinary causes that commonly produce UFO sightings. The Great Orme’s coastal setting creates several opportunities for mistaken identification:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* **Aircraft and helicopters:** Coastal routes and training areas can produce lights that appear unusual when viewed from below or at a distance.
* **Ships and maritime lights:** From elevated viewpoints such as the Great Orme, lights on vessels can appear detached from the horizon or seem to move strangely.
* **Astronomical objects:** Bright planets and stars close to the horizon can appear to change position or behaviour because of atmospheric distortion.
* **Weather and atmospheric effects:** Mist, haze and reflections over the sea can alter the appearance of distant lights.

</div>

These explanations do not automatically account for every individual report. A particular witness may have seen something that remains unidentified because the available information is incomplete. However, “unidentified” is a description of the evidence available, not a conclusion about the object’s origin. ## What Llandudno adds to Caernarfonshire’s UFO history

Llandudno’s importance lies less in one definitive incident and more in how it represents a regional pattern. The Great Orme became a recognised setting for UFO claims because its dramatic landscape encouraged observation, storytelling and repeated interest from local enthusiasts. It sits within the wider North Wales tradition of unusual aerial reports, where famous cases and smaller local sightings have contributed to the area’s reputation.

For researchers, the Llandudno reports are valuable because they show the difference between a reported event and a proven event.
