## Introduction

On 4 February 1977, pupils at Broad Haven Primary School in [Pembrokeshire]({{ 'what-really-happened-in-pembrokeshires/' | relative_url }}) reported seeing an unfamiliar silver object in fields beyond the playground. The most defensible conclusion is narrow but important: several children appear sincerely to have seen, or believed they saw, something unusual in the same general place. Their early accounts and drawings preserve a real reported event, not merely a story invented decades later. They do not, however, establish that a spacecraft landed or that its occupants were extraterrestrial.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://feeds.bbci.co.uk/news/uk-wales-south-west-wales-38723643" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: feeds.bbci.co.uk">[bbci.co.uk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">feeds.bbci.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">BBC FeedsBroad Haven UFO sightings marked 40 years on - BBC NewsFebruary 4, 2017 — Broad Haven UFO sightings marked 40 years on - BBC New...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 4, 2017</span></span></span>

The case matters within Pembrokeshire’s UFO history because it became the opening and best-documented episode of the 1977 Broad Haven wave. Its strength lies in the number of witnesses, the relatively prompt collection of their recollections and the survival of their drawings. ## The events of 4 February 1977

The sighting took place during the school day at Broad Haven, a coastal village overlooking St Brides Bay in the historic county of Pembrokeshire. Published accounts do not agree perfectly on the number involved, commonly giving fourteen or fifteen pupils. That uncertainty is a useful warning against treating later summaries as if they were a single, complete official record.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://feeds.bbci.co.uk/news/uk-wales-south-west-wales-38723643" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: feeds.bbci.co.uk">[bbci.co.uk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">feeds.bbci.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">BBC FeedsBroad Haven UFO sightings marked 40 years on - BBC NewsFebruary 4, 2017 — Broad Haven UFO sightings marked 40 years on - BBC New...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 4, 2017</span></span></span>

The central claim is consistent across most versions. Children said that an object appeared in or beyond fields near the school boundary. It was usually described as silver, metallic or silvery-green, with a long, flattened, cigar-like or disc-like body and a raised dome. Some accounts included a flashing or coloured light. A smaller group reported a pale or silver-clothed figure near the object, but the humanoid element was not shared by every witness and should not be treated as part of an identical group account.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://feeds.bbci.co.uk/news/uk-wales-south-west-wales-38723643" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: feeds.bbci.co.uk">[bbci.co.uk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">feeds.bbci.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">BBC FeedsBroad Haven UFO sightings marked 40 years on - BBC NewsFebruary 4, 2017 — Broad Haven UFO sightings marked 40 years on - BBC New...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 4, 2017</span></span></span>

The children did not all observe the scene together in one continuous viewing. Reports appear to have spread among pupils during the day, with children going to the boundary or looking towards the field at different times. David Davies, then aged about ten, later said that he initially dismissed the other pupils’ excitement and went to look because he expected to prove them wrong. He recalled briefly seeing a silver, cigar-shaped object rise into view near trees and then drop out of sight. That makes his testimony interesting, but not fully independent: before looking, he already knew that classmates were discussing a supposed flying saucer and had some idea of where to look.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://feeds.bbci.co.uk/news/uk-wales-south-west-wales-38723643" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: feeds.bbci.co.uk">[bbci.co.uk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">feeds.bbci.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">BBC FeedsBroad Haven UFO sightings marked 40 years on - BBC NewsFebruary 4, 2017 — Broad Haven UFO sightings marked 40 years on - BBC New...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 4, 2017</span></span></span>

The school’s response is frequently compressed into the claim that the pupils were immediately separated and produced matching drawings. The surviving narrative is more complicated. Headteacher Ralph Llewellyn did collect individual accounts and drawings, reportedly under separated or controlled conditions, but the formal exercise is generally dated to Monday 7 February, after the Friday sighting and a weekend in which the children had opportunities to talk with friends and families. Separation prevented copying during the drawing session; it could not restore complete independence to memories already discussed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://unidentifiedphenomena.com/incidents/the-broad-haven-ufo-incident-1977/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: unidentifiedphenomena.com">[unidentifiedphenomena.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">unidentifiedphenomena.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">the broad haven ufo incident 1977</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Unidentified PhenomenaThe Broad Haven UFO Incident (1977) – Unidentified PhenomenaThe Broad Haven UFO Incident (1977) – Unidentified Phen...</span></span></span>

## What the drawings and testimony show

The drawings are the case’s most memorable physical record. They show that a number of pupils associated the experience with the same broad visual idea: a horizontally extended object, commonly with a central dome or raised upper section. This shared structure is more significant than an isolated child’s sketch because it supports the claim that a common subject, description or group concept existed by the time the drawings were made.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.myjoyonline.com/alien-and-ufo-bermuda-triangle-explored-in-new-netflix-documentary/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: myjoyonline.com">[myjoyonline.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">myjoyonline.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Mr Llewellyn later told the BBC&#x27;s children&#x27;s current affairs show John Craven&#x27;s Newsround that all the descriptions were &quot;similar&quot;.... They are not, however, near-identical technical diagrams. Reproductions show differences in outline, proportion, colour, windows, lights, supporting details and the position or appearance of any figure. Some resemble a cigar with a dome; others look more saucer-like. Those variations are not proof that the witnesses fabricated the story. People observing an unfamiliar object from different positions and for different lengths of time would not be expected to produce matching pictures. But the differences do weaken the popular claim that every child independently recorded exactly the same craft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://feeds.bbci.co.uk/news/uk-wales-south-west-wales-38723643" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: feeds.bbci.co.uk">[bbci.co.uk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">feeds.bbci.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">BBC FeedsBroad Haven UFO sightings marked 40 years on - BBC NewsFebruary 4, 2017 — Broad Haven UFO sightings marked 40 years on - BBC New...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 4, 2017</span></span></span>

A child’s drawing is also not equivalent to a photograph. It mixes visual memory with drawing ability, familiar visual categories and decisions about which features seem important. Research on children’s drawings and testimony finds that drawings may preserve useful information but do not necessarily contain the same details as verbal reports, and their contents can change across interviews. The Broad Haven sketches therefore demonstrate what the pupils represented on paper after the event; they cannot securely establish the object’s precise dimensions, distance, surface, movement or mechanical construction.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/pdf/10.1177/02762374211047971" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: journals.sagepub.com">[Sage Journals]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">journals.sagepub.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on sagepub.com.</span></span></span>

The written and spoken recollections support a similar limited conclusion. Multiple pupils reported a bright or metallic-looking object in the same direction, but important particulars were unevenly distributed. Not every child reported movement. Not every child saw a figure. Estimates of size depended on an uncertain distance, while trees, bushes and the school boundary partly restricted the view. The accounts support a shared unusual sighting more strongly than they support a single detailed reconstruction of what physically occupied the field.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://unidentifiedphenomena.com/incidents/the-broad-haven-ufo-incident-1977/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: unidentifiedphenomena.com">[unidentifiedphenomena.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">unidentifiedphenomena.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">the broad haven ufo incident 1977</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Unidentified PhenomenaThe Broad Haven UFO Incident (1977) – Unidentified PhenomenaThe Broad Haven UFO Incident (1977) – Unidentified Phen...</span></span></span>

Sincerity is one of the case’s strongest human features. Davies and other former pupils continued to describe the event long after the original publicity, despite ridicule rather than obvious personal advantage. Davies has said that association with the sighting contributed to serious bullying. Persistence under those circumstances counts against the simplest suggestion that every witness knowingly invented the story for amusement. It does not settle whether the original perception was accurate: an honest witness can remain sincerely convinced of a mistaken interpretation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://feeds.bbci.co.uk/news/uk-wales-south-west-wales-38723643" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: feeds.bbci.co.uk">[BBC Feeds]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">feeds.bbci.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">BBC FeedsBroad Haven UFO sightings marked 40 years on - BBC NewsFebruary 4, 2017 — Broad Haven UFO sightings marked 40 years on - BBC New...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 4, 2017</span></span></span>

## Why sincere witnesses can still be mistaken

The pupils’ age does not make their evidence worthless. Modern research does not support the crude idea that children are inherently incapable of reliable observation. Children can report genuine experiences accurately, especially when they are allowed to describe them freely and are questioned promptly without pressure. Their reliability, like that of adults, depends on viewing conditions, the delay before formal questioning, the wording of questions and exposure to information from other people.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.repository.cam.ac.uk/bitstreams/7e659cb7-0114-4f1f-9d39-845228f9022d/download" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: repository.cam.ac.uk">[Cambridge University Repository]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">repository.cam.ac.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on cam.ac.uk.</span></span></span>

Broad Haven contains both favourable and unfavourable conditions. The reports concerned a striking event in daylight and were recorded within days, not first recovered decades later. On the other hand, the children were classmates, heard one another’s claims and had a weekend before the best-known drawing exercise. Eyewitness research describes “memory conformity”, in which discussion with co-witnesses can alter which details people remember or how confidently they report them. This can happen without deliberate lying: information supplied by another witness may gradually feel like part of one’s own observation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.repository.cam.ac.uk/bitstreams/7e659cb7-0114-4f1f-9d39-845228f9022d/download" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: repository.cam.ac.uk">[Cambridge University Repository]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">repository.cam.ac.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on cam.ac.uk.</span></span></span>

Group influence does not require every child to copy a leader. One pupil may identify a distant shape as a “flying saucer”; others then look towards the same place with that expectation. A partially obscured tank, farm equipment, vehicle, aircraft or reflection can consequently be interpreted through the shared label. Later discussion may standardise a few prominent features—silver colour, elongated body, dome—while leaving the considerable differences seen in the individual drawings. This is a plausible mechanism, not proof that it occurred.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.higgypop.com/news/uncanny-the-ufo-in-the-playground/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: higgypop.com">[higgypop.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">higgypop.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Todmorden UFO Might Be Linked To Broad Haven &amp; Zimbabwe Sightings</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Todmorden UFO Might Be Linked To Broad Haven &amp; Zimbabwe Sightings</span></span></span>

Distance is another central problem. Some reconstructions place the scene several hundred yards from the pupils, beyond a fence, stream and vegetation. At that range, reliable judgement of size depends on knowing the distance, while detail can be inferred from silhouette, glare and expectation. A fixed cylindrical object with a raised fitting has sometimes been suggested, including a sewage or slurry tank. The proposal fits the general cigar-and-dome outline but does not readily explain testimony that the object rose or moved, unless that motion was apparent, involved a vehicle behind uneven ground, or became sharper in later memory.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://feeds.bbci.co.uk/news/uk-wales-south-west-wales-38723643" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: feeds.bbci.co.uk">[BBC Feeds]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">feeds.bbci.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">BBC FeedsBroad Haven UFO sightings marked 40 years on - BBC NewsFebruary 4, 2017 — Broad Haven UFO sightings marked 40 years on - BBC New...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 4, 2017</span></span></span>

A hoax has also been proposed, particularly for the reports of silver-clothed figures elsewhere in the 1977 Pembrokeshire wave. Official correspondence later quoted by the BBC referred to local speculation about a practical joker, and an RAF officer noted that descriptions of protective clothing resembled suits used for industrial fire emergencies. Glyn Edwards subsequently claimed that he had worn a silver suit as a prank during the period. This gives a credible explanation for at least some humanoid reports, but it does not by itself demonstrate that he staged the school sighting or produced the distant object seen on 4 February.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://feeds.bbci.co.uk/news/uk-wales-south-west-wales-38723643" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: feeds.bbci.co.uk">[BBC Feeds]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">feeds.bbci.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">BBC FeedsBroad Haven UFO sightings marked 40 years on - BBC NewsFebruary 4, 2017 — Broad Haven UFO sightings marked 40 years on - BBC New...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 4, 2017</span></span></span>

Military or aviation activity is likewise relevant without being a complete solution. RAF Brawdy was close enough to make aircraft and service activity part of the local visual environment, and later theories have invoked Harrier aircraft or military protective clothing. Yet no publicly available flight log, radar record or contemporary service document has been shown to match a specific aircraft to the school’s observation at the necessary time and position. Aviation remains a reasonable category of explanation rather than an evidenced identification of the object.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://feeds.bbci.co.uk/news/uk-wales-south-west-wales-38723643" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: feeds.bbci.co.uk">[BBC Feeds]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">feeds.bbci.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">BBC FeedsBroad Haven UFO sightings marked 40 years on - BBC NewsFebruary 4, 2017 — Broad Haven UFO sightings marked 40 years on - BBC New...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 4, 2017</span></span></span>

## Did investigation strengthen the case?

The immediate school procedure strengthened the historical record more than it strengthened the extraordinary interpretation. Llewellyn preserved separate drawings and statements rather than allowing the event to survive only as playground rumour. Contemporary television and newspaper coverage also recorded some of the pupils while they were still children. These materials establish that the core report existed in 1977 and allow later versions to be checked against earlier ones.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://feeds.bbci.co.uk/news/uk-wales-south-west-wales-38723643" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: feeds.bbci.co.uk">[bbci.co.uk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">feeds.bbci.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">BBC FeedsBroad Haven UFO sightings marked 40 years on - BBC NewsFebruary 4, 2017 — Broad Haven UFO sightings marked 40 years on - BBC New...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 4, 2017</span></span></span>

Civilian UFO investigators later examined the school testimony as part of the wider Pembrokeshire reports. Their work helped document names, drawings and chronology, but it did not produce decisive physical evidence. The school event itself yielded no verified photograph, material sample or instrument reading. Reports of scorched ground and an RAF site visit relate principally to a later incident near the Haven Fort Hotel, not to physical confirmation of the pupils’ object. Merging those separate episodes makes the school evidence appear stronger than it is.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://feeds.bbci.co.uk/news/uk-wales-south-west-wales-38723643" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: feeds.bbci.co.uk">[BBC Feeds]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">feeds.bbci.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">BBC FeedsBroad Haven UFO sightings marked 40 years on - BBC NewsFebruary 4, 2017 — Broad Haven UFO sightings marked 40 years on - BBC New...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 4, 2017</span></span></span>

Government interest has also been exaggerated in some retellings. The then Pembroke MP, Nicholas Edwards, contacted the Ministry of Defence after receiving numerous reports from constituents, and official staff considered whether local pranksters might be involved. That correspondence shows that authorities were aware of the regional controversy. It does not show that the Ministry of Defence authenticated a landing at Broad Haven School. Official attention can establish that a report was taken, discussed or passed between departments; it is not equivalent to official confirmation of its cause.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://feeds.bbci.co.uk/news/uk-wales-south-west-wales-38723643" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: feeds.bbci.co.uk">[BBC Feeds]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">feeds.bbci.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">BBC FeedsBroad Haven UFO sightings marked 40 years on - BBC NewsFebruary 4, 2017 — Broad Haven UFO sightings marked 40 years on - BBC New...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 4, 2017</span></span></span>

Later television programmes have preserved valuable witness interviews but have also placed the school episode inside a much larger narrative of lights, humanoids, government secrecy and sightings across south-west Wales. The BBC’s 2024 series revisited the witnesses and considered both conventional and extraordinary possibilities, while Netflix’s *Encounters* brought the story to an international audience in 2023. Such programmes strengthen public access to the testimony, but their dramatic structure can blur distinctions between evidence from 4 February and reports made weeks or months later by different people.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://genome.ch.bbc.co.uk/m00203ln" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: genome.ch.bbc.co.uk">[bbc.co.uk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">genome.ch.bbc.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">BBC Genome Episode</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">BBC Genome Episode</span></span></span>

Retrospective interviews add information about the witnesses’ experience and the lasting social impact of the case, but they are weaker for recovering small observational details. Memory is reconstructive, and repeated interviews can reinforce a coherent narrative while obscuring earlier uncertainty. The most evidentially valuable materials remain the pupils’ earliest drawings, statements and contemporary recordings—not because they are infallible, but because they are closest in time to the event.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.repository.cam.ac.uk/bitstreams/7e659cb7-0114-4f1f-9d39-845228f9022d/download" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: repository.cam.ac.uk">[cam.ac.uk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">repository.cam.ac.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on cam.ac.uk.</span></span></span>

## What can reasonably be concluded?

The Broad Haven School sighting is stronger than a late, anonymous UFO anecdote. It involved multiple identifiable witnesses at a known place and time, and their accounts were recorded while the event was recent. The surviving material supports the conclusion that a group of pupils reported an unfamiliar silver, dome-topped or elongated object beyond the school grounds. The witnesses’ continued testimony gives good reason to treat their experience respectfully and not dismiss it as a knowingly invented tale.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://feeds.bbci.co.uk/news/uk-wales-south-west-wales-38723643" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: feeds.bbci.co.uk">[bbci.co.uk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">feeds.bbci.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">BBC FeedsBroad Haven UFO sightings marked 40 years on - BBC NewsFebruary 4, 2017 — Broad Haven UFO sightings marked 40 years on - BBC New...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 4, 2017</span></span></span>

The evidence does not determine what they saw. The witnesses were socially connected; discussion preceded formal recording; the drawings agree in broad outline but differ in detail; and the object was distant and partly obscured. No independent adult is securely documented as observing the same object at the same time, and no physical or instrumental evidence closes the identification gap.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.higgypop.com/news/uncanny-the-ufo-in-the-playground/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: higgypop.com">[higgypop.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">higgypop.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Todmorden UFO Might Be Linked To Broad Haven &amp; Zimbabwe Sightings</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Todmorden UFO Might Be Linked To Broad Haven &amp; Zimbabwe Sightings</span></span></span>

The fairest classification is therefore **unresolved but evidentially limited**. Misidentified machinery, a vehicle or aircraft, an optical effect, group expectation and memory conformity are all plausible components. A prank may explain some associated figure reports, though there is no firm demonstration that a prankster created the school event itself. An extraterrestrial craft remains an interpretation rather than a conclusion supported by the available evidence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://feeds.bbci.co.uk/news/uk-wales-south-west-wales-38723643" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: feeds.bbci.co.uk">[bbci.co.uk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">feeds.bbci.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">BBC FeedsBroad Haven UFO sightings marked 40 years on - BBC NewsFebruary 4, 2017 — Broad Haven UFO sightings marked 40 years on - BBC New...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 4, 2017</span></span></span>

Within Pembrokeshire’s UFO history, the sighting’s lasting importance is not that it proves alien visitation. It is that Broad Haven preserves an unusually rich record of how a sincere group experience can become evidence, controversy and local folklore at the same time. The drawings make the case more substantial than rumour, while their differences, the delay before collection and the lack of independent confirmation prevent the story from becoming proof.
