## Introduction

The Dechmont Woods encounter remains [West Lothian]({{ 'what-really-happened-in-west-lothian/' | relative_url }})’s most debated UFO case because it contains something unusual in British sighting history: not only a close witness report, but also a police investigation, alleged physical traces, and damaged clothing examined after the event. On 9 November 1979, forestry worker Robert “Bob” Taylor reported encountering a large dome-shaped object in woodland near Livingston, followed by two smaller objects that appeared to attach themselves to his clothing. The case became famous because the incident was treated locally as a possible assault rather than simply recorded as an unusual sighting.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Taylor_incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Robert Taylor incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Robert Taylor incident</span></span></span>



<img src="{{ "/assets/images/West_Lothian_1c9504_dechmont_woods_case_2d5b2c-overview.webp" | relative_url }}" alt="Overview image for Dechmont Woods" loading="eager" decoding="sync" fetchpriority="high">

The evidence has never produced a confirmed explanation. Supporters argue that Taylor’s reputation, his immediate report to his family, the police involvement, and the physical marks found at the site make the case difficult to dismiss. Sceptics counter that the evidence does not prove an extraordinary cause, that no independent witness saw the object, and that several conventional interpretations remain possible. The lasting importance of Dechmont Woods is therefore not that it proves a UFO visit, but that it sits in the difficult space between a credible personal experience and an unresolved physical mystery.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.westlothian.gov.uk/media/26988/Dechmont-Law-UFO-info/pdf/Dechmont_Law_UFO.pdf?m=1575475239773" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: westlothian.gov.uk">[West Lothian Council]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">westlothian.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">West Lothian Council Top secret</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">West Lothian Council Top secret</span></span></span>

## Robert Taylor’s reported encounter

### A close encounter with unusual physical claims

Taylor, a forestry worker employed by the Livingston Development Corporation, said he was carrying out routine work near Dechmont Law when he entered a clearing with his dog. He reported seeing a large, dark, dome-like object close to the ground. According to his account, two smaller spherical objects with protruding features moved towards him, appeared to pull at his trousers, and caused him to lose consciousness. When he recovered, he said the main object had gone and that he was left shaken, injured, and with torn clothing.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Taylor_incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Robert Taylor incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Robert Taylor incident</span></span></span>

The detail that made the case stand out was not simply the claimed object, but the sequence of events afterwards. Taylor did not immediately present the story as a public UFO claim; instead, after returning home distressed, he reported that he had been attacked. Police became involved because the torn clothing and his condition raised the possibility of an ordinary assault. This created an unusual situation in which officers investigated the aftermath of an alleged encounter without accepting the explanation that Taylor himself later favoured.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufoinsight.com/ufos/close-encounters/livingston-dechmont-woods-criminal-investigation-ufo" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufoinsight.com">[UFO Insight]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufoinsight.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO InsightThe Livingston Dechmont Woods Incident – A &quot;Criminal Investigation&quot; Of A UFO! - UFO InsightMarch 28, 2017...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 28, 2017</span></span></span>

Taylor’s personal credibility has remained central to the debate. Those who accept the case as significant often point to his reputation as a respected local worker and the fact that he maintained the same account for the rest of his life. Critics generally do not argue that Taylor invented the story deliberately, but instead focus on whether a sincere witness can misinterpret an unusual experience, especially when the available physical evidence is incomplete.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.westlothian.gov.uk/media/26988/Dechmont-Law-UFO-info/pdf/Dechmont_Law_UFO.pdf?m=1575475239773" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: westlothian.gov.uk">[West Lothian Council]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">westlothian.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">West Lothian Council Top secret</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">West Lothian Council Top secret</span></span></span>

## Physical traces and police investigation

### The marks that made Dechmont Woods different

The strongest argument for the case’s importance comes from the reported scene evidence. Investigators recorded unusual marks in the clearing, including a pattern of holes and track-like impressions. Some accounts describe approximately forty marks and compare parts of the pattern to machinery tracks, although interpretations of what created them remain disputed. The police investigation did not establish what caused the markings.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufologie.patrickgross.org/htm/dechmontwoods79.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[ufologie.patrickgross.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ufo - UFOs at close sight: Dechmont Woods, Scotland, November 9, 1979...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 9, 1979</span></span></span>

Taylor’s trousers were also treated as evidence. Because the matter was investigated as a possible assault, clothing was sent for examination. The reported conclusion was that the damage was consistent with a sharp upward pulling action, but this finding did not identify the cause of the tears. Supporters see the damaged clothing as one of the case’s most important features because it links Taylor’s account to a physical event. Sceptics argue that damage to clothing, while unusual, does not demonstrate that the cause was an unknown craft or non-human mechanism.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.westlothian.gov.uk/media/26988/Dechmont-Law-UFO-info/pdf/Dechmont_Law_UFO.pdf?m=1575475239773" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: westlothian.gov.uk">[West Lothian Council]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">westlothian.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">West Lothian Council Top secret</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">West Lothian Council Top secret</span></span></span>

The police response is often described as a major reason the case remains famous. Officers examined the site rather than simply recording a strange report, and some investigators reportedly remained uncertain about the origin of the marks. However, uncertainty is not the same as confirmation: a police investigation can establish that something happened without proving the explanation proposed by a witness.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufologie.patrickgross.org/htm/dechmontwoods79.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[ufologie.patrickgross.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ufo - UFOs at close sight: Dechmont Woods, Scotland, November 9, 1979...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 9, 1979</span></span></span>

### What the physical evidence can and cannot show

The evidence from Dechmont Woods has strengths and limitations.

**What supports the case:**


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Taylor’s report was made close to the time of the alleged event rather than years later.
* The incident involved physical details beyond a distant light in the sky.
* Police attention and examination of the scene provide a stronger record than many anecdotal UFO reports.
* Taylor’s account remained consistent over many years.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Taylor_incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Robert Taylor incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Robert Taylor incident</span></span></span>

</div>

**What limits the case:**


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* There was only one direct witness to the alleged object.
* No object, material sample, or independently verifiable technology was recovered.
* The marks were recorded, but their origin was never conclusively established.
* The physical evidence does not uniquely identify an extraordinary explanation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.westlothian.gov.uk/media/26988/Dechmont-Law-UFO-info/pdf/Dechmont_Law_UFO.pdf?m=1575475239773" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: westlothian.gov.uk">[West Lothian Council]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">westlothian.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">West Lothian Council Top secret</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">West Lothian Council Top secret</span></span></span>

</div>

This distinction explains why the case remains unresolved rather than proven. The evidence suggests an unusual event occurred, but it does not settle what that event was.

## Arguments for and against explanations

### The case for an unexplained encounter

Supporters of the Dechmont Woods case argue that ordinary explanations struggle to account for the combination of details. They point especially to the relationship between Taylor’s reported injuries, the torn trousers, and the marks in the clearing. For them, the case is stronger than typical UFO reports because the witness did not simply describe something unusual in the sky; he described a physical interaction that left apparent traces.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.thinkaboutitdocs.com/1979-the-dechmont-woods-encounter/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: thinkaboutitdocs.com">[Think About It Docs]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">thinkaboutitdocs.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">1979 the dechmont woods encounter</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Think About It Docs1979: The Dechmont Woods Encounter...</span></span></span>

Another argument concerns Taylor himself. Many researchers who favour the case have emphasised that he was not a publicity-seeking figure and did not appear to gain obvious benefit from making the claim. ### Conventional explanations and sceptical challenges

Sceptical explanations have focused on whether the event could have involved a misidentification, a natural phenomenon, a psychological episode, or an ordinary object interpreted in unusual circumstances. The most discussed alternative came from writer Steuart Campbell, who argued that Taylor may have experienced an atmospheric mirage involving astronomical objects such as Venus, combined with a physical or perceptual reaction. Campbell’s theory became one of the best-known attempts to explain the case without invoking an unknown craft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.steuartcampbell.com/ufos.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: steuartcampbell.com">[Steuart Campbell]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">steuartcampbell.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Steuart Campbell UFOs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Steuart CampbellUFOs - Steuart Campbell...</span></span></span>

The mirage explanation has been controversial because critics argue that it does not easily explain every part of Taylor’s report, especially the alleged attack by smaller objects and the physical traces. Even some UFO researchers who rejected an extraterrestrial interpretation considered the explanation incomplete rather than a final solution.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufologie.patrickgross.org/htm/dechmontwoods79.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[ufologie.patrickgross.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ufo - UFOs at close sight: Dechmont Woods, Scotland, November 9, 1979...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 9, 1979</span></span></span>

Other sceptical arguments focus on the limits of the investigation itself. The fact that police could not identify the source of the marks does not necessarily mean the cause was extraordinary; it may also reflect the difficulty of reconstructing an outdoor event after the fact. Woodland environments can contain many possible sources of disturbance, and the available records cannot recreate every condition present at the time.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufologie.patrickgross.org/htm/dechmontwoods79.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[ufologie.patrickgross.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ufo - UFOs at close sight: Dechmont Woods, Scotland, November 9, 1979...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 9, 1979</span></span></span>

## Why the Dechmont Woods debate continues

The enduring mystery of Dechmont Woods comes from a tension between credibility and proof. The case is stronger than many UFO stories because it includes a named witness, contemporary reporting, police involvement, and claimed physical evidence. At the same time, it falls short of the type of evidence that would allow a firm conclusion, such as independent observations, recoverable material evidence, or a clear demonstration that known explanations cannot apply.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Taylor_incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Robert Taylor incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Robert Taylor incident</span></span></span>

Within West Lothian’s UFO history, the encounter remains important because it represents a rare example of a close-range report with an investigative trail. The debate has shifted over time from “did Taylor experience something?” to the harder question of “what best explains what happened?” That distinction is why Dechmont Woods remains unresolved: many people accept that Taylor experienced a genuine and unusual event, while disagreement continues over the nature of that event.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.westlothian.gov.uk/media/26988/Dechmont-Law-UFO-info/pdf/Dechmont_Law_UFO.pdf?m=1575475239773" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: westlothian.gov.uk">[West Lothian Council]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">westlothian.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">West Lothian Council Top secret</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">West Lothian Council Top secret</span></span></span>

  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
