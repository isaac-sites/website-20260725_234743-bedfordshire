## Introduction

RAF Coningsby is one of the most important reasons [Lincolnshire]({{ 'lincolnshires-ufo-mysteries-cases-clues/' | relative_url }}) has a continuing association with unusual aerial reports. The base is not known because of a single proven UFO incident, but because it sits at the centre of a busy military aviation environment where unfamiliar aircraft, training activity, air-defence operations and restricted airspace can make ordinary events appear unusual. RAF Coningsby is a Quick Reaction Alert (QRA) station responsible for protecting UK airspace and is home to Typhoon fighter operations, making it one of the most visible military aviation sites in eastern England.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.raf.mod.uk/our-organisation/stations/raf-coningsby/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: raf.mod.uk">[Royal Air Force]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">raf.mod.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Royal Air Force RAF Coningsby &#124; Royal Air Force</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Royal Air Force RAF Coningsby &#124; Royal Air Force</span></span></span>

## The base and its aviation role

RAF Coningsby’s military importance explains why unusual sightings are repeatedly associated with the area. The station, located in Lincolnshire near the villages of Coningsby and Tattershall, has a long history of operating frontline aircraft. It was originally built for Bomber Command, later became associated with aircraft such as the Phantom and Tornado, and now operates the Eurofighter Typhoon. It is also home to the Battle of Britain Memorial Flight, adding historic aircraft movements to the range of aviation seen in the area.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/noise-amelioration-scheme-military-raf-coningsby" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: GOV.UK">[GOV.UK]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">GOV.UK</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Noise Amelioration Scheme (Military): RAF Coningsby</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Noise Amelioration Scheme (Military): RAF Coningsby</span></span></span>

For UFO reporting, this creates a particular challenge. A rural observer near Coningsby may see fast jets at unusual angles, night flying, aircraft using afterburners, formation training or movements connected with readiness duties. A sighting can therefore be entirely genuine in the sense that a person really saw something unusual, while the explanation may still be a normal military aircraft.

The base’s role as a QRA station is especially important. QRA aircraft are maintained ready for rapid response, meaning military activity around the station can include flights that are not part of ordinary public air displays or routine passenger aviation. RAF Coningsby hosts Typhoon squadrons and training activity, including No. 29 Squadron, which trains combat air pilots on the Typhoon.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.raf.mod.uk/our-organisation/squadrons/29-squadron/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: raf.mod.uk">[Royal Air Force]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">raf.mod.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Royal Air Force29 Squadron &#124; Royal Air Force</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Royal Air Force29 Squadron &#124; Royal Air Force</span></span></span>

## Recorded sightings near Coningsby

One of the clearest examples of the type of report associated with the area is a 2009 sighting recorded in Ministry of Defence UFO files. A report from Tattershall, close to RAF Coningsby, described three orange lights seen for around 40 minutes, apparently hovering and later disappearing. The report stated that it was observed from Tattershall Castle and reported to Lincolnshire Police.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://documents.theblackvault.com/documents/ukufo/defe-24-2464-1.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: documents.theblackvault.com">[documents.theblackvault.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">documents.theblackvault.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">MOD Form 262FApril 14, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 14, 2026</span></span></span>

The importance of this report is not that it proves an unexplained craft was present, but that it shows the kind of observation that entered official records. The details preserved in MoD files often describe what witnesses saw, where they were located and environmental circumstances, but they do not automatically establish the cause. The National Archives notes that many UFO reports contain descriptions of lights, shapes and flashes that can later be explained by known objects or atmospheric effects.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/explore-the-collection/explore-by-time-period/postwar/ufo-reports/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nationalarchives.gov.uk">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nationalarchives.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National Archives UFO reports</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesUFO reports - The National Archives...</span></span></span>

The Coningsby area also illustrates why military locations frequently generate reports. A witness may correctly identify a military connection — for example, hearing a jet engine or seeing a fighter aircraft — but misunderstand the purpose of the flight. Without access to operational information, an observer cannot know whether an aircraft is conducting training, interception practice, navigation exercises or another military task.

## Aircraft explanations and uncertainty

The most common explanation for Coningsby-related UFO reports is not that witnesses saw nothing, but that they saw something real and interpreted it incorrectly. Military aircraft create several identification difficulties:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* **Speed and distance:** A Typhoon fighter can appear to move suddenly because of its speed, altitude and changing angle relative to the observer.
* **Night operations:** Navigation lights, engine glow and afterburner effects can look unlike familiar aircraft.
* **Limited information:** Military flights may not appear on public tracking systems, leaving observers without an easy way to confirm what they saw.
* **Unusual flight patterns:** Training routes, intercept exercises and operational flying can look unlike normal civilian aircraft movements.

</div>

RAF Coningsby’s own guidance reflects the intensity of aviation activity around the station. The RAF states that low flying is an essential skill for aircrew and provides information about aircraft activity and reporting concerns. The station also highlights the safety and security issues created by unauthorised drones near military aircraft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.raf.mod.uk/our-organisation/stations/raf-coningsby/flying-info/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: raf.mod.uk">[Royal Air Force]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">raf.mod.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Royal Air Force Flying Info &#124; RAF Coningsby &#124; Royal Air Force</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Royal Air Force Flying Info &#124; RAF Coningsby &#124; Royal Air Force</span></span></span>

Drones have added a newer category of potential explanation and concern. Unlike traditional UFO reports involving mysterious lights, drones can represent a genuine aviation-security issue because small unmanned aircraft may be difficult to identify visually and can operate near restricted areas. RAF Coningsby advises that suspicious unmanned aircraft activity around the station should be reported to RAF Police.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.raf.mod.uk/our-organisation/stations/raf-coningsby/flying-info/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: raf.mod.uk">[Royal Air Force]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">raf.mod.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Royal Air Force Flying Info &#124; RAF Coningsby &#124; Royal Air Force</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Royal Air Force Flying Info &#124; RAF Coningsby &#124; Royal Air Force</span></span></span>

## Why Coningsby remains important in Lincolnshire UFO history

RAF Coningsby matters because it demonstrates the wider pattern behind many Lincolnshire aerial reports: places with significant military activity naturally produce more observations. A major fighter station places advanced aircraft, training activity and restricted operations into the same skies watched by local residents.

The evidence around Coningsby is therefore stronger as a study of aviation uncertainty than as evidence for extraordinary explanations. Some reports remain unresolved because there is not enough information to identify the object or event. Others are likely linked to aircraft activity, but cannot always be confirmed publicly because military operations are not fully disclosed.

The most useful distinction is between an **unidentified observation** and an **unidentified object of unknown origin**. Coningsby provides many examples of the first category: people reporting something unusual in a complex military airspace environment.     return encodeURIComponent(String(value || '').replace(/\s+/g, ' ').trim()).replace(/%20/g, '+');
  }
  function buildUrl(query, reference, marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket];
    if (!cfg) return '#';
    var template = String(cfg.url_template || 'https://www.ebay.com/sch/i.html?_nkw={query}');
    var url = template.replace('{query}', encodeQuery(query || '')).replace('{query_plain}', String(query || ''));
    var joiner = url.indexOf('?') >= 0 ? '&' : '?';
    var params = [];
    if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
</script>
