## Introduction

The Avoch mystery is a small but revealing example of how older Highland folklore can become connected with modern UFO discussions. The story usually described as the “1909 Avoch encounter” does not involve a documented flying object report, an official investigation, or contemporary newspaper evidence of an unknown craft. Instead, it concerns a later retelling of an alleged encounter at Rosehaugh House near Avoch, on the Black Isle, in which two children were said to have seen two small human-like figures inside the house.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufologie.patrickgross.org/ce3/1909-uk-avoch.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[Ufologie]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOS at close sight: URECAT-001496 - 1909, Avoch, Ross and Cromarty, U-K., twin boysApril 1, 2013...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 1, 2013</span></span></span>

Its importance within the UFO history of historic [Cromartyshire]({{ 'what-really-lies-behind-cromartyshire/' | relative_url }}) lies less in proving an unexplained event and more in showing the difficulties of using folklore as evidence. The account sits at the boundary between local tradition, fairy-lore, paranormal writing, and later UFO classification. The available evidence suggests a story preserved through later sources rather than a well-recorded 1909 incident.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufologie.patrickgross.org/ce3/1909-uk-avoch.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[Ufologie]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOS at close sight: URECAT-001496 - 1909, Avoch, Ross and Cromarty, U-K., twin boysApril 1, 2013...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 1, 2013</span></span></span>

## The claimed 1909 account

The Avoch story is generally traced to a claim that, in 1909, nine-year-old twin boys, described as the sons of a gamekeeper, were at Rosehaugh House near Avoch when they encountered two unusually small figures on the staircase. Later versions describe the figures as around 18 inches tall, with grey hair and beards, wearing loose clothing and small pointed hats. The figures were said to pass the children and disappear into the hall.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufologie.patrickgross.org/ce3/1909-uk-avoch.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[Ufologie]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOS at close sight: URECAT-001496 - 1909, Avoch, Ross and Cromarty, U-K., twin boysApril 1, 2013...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 1, 2013</span></span></span>

The location adds to the story’s appeal. Rosehaugh House was a real estate on the Black Isle, and local historical records place important changes at the property around this period, including the completion of major remodelling work in the early 1900s.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://avoch.org/history/timeline" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: avoch.org">[avoch.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">avoch.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Timeline – Avoch&#x27;s heritage</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Timeline – Avoch&#x27;s heritage</span></span></span> A genuine historic setting can make later legends feel more convincing, but the existence of a real place does not confirm that the claimed event occurred.

A further detail sometimes repeated in retellings is the discovery of a strange bootlace-like leather strip without metal fastenings. This element appears in later summaries of the account rather than in a surviving contemporary investigation or verified record.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufologie.patrickgross.org/ce3/1909-uk-avoch.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[Ufologie]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOS at close sight: URECAT-001496 - 1909, Avoch, Ross and Cromarty, U-K., twin boysApril 1, 2013...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 1, 2013</span></span></span>

The year 1909 also has a broader place in the history of unusual reports. Britain and other countries experienced a wave of “mystery airship” stories around this time, with people reporting strange objects in the sky before aviation was widely understood by the public.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFO_sightings_in_the_United_Kingdom" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO sightings in the United Kingdom</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO sightings in the United Kingdom</span></span></span> However, the Avoch story is different in character: it is an alleged indoor encounter with small figures rather than a sighting of an aerial object.

## Later retellings and sources

The main difficulty with the Avoch account is the gap between the claimed date and the surviving sources. The story is commonly linked to later writers of folklore and fairy-related material rather than to a 1909 police report, newspaper investigation, scientific inquiry, or official archive. One catalogue of the case notes that the earliest known reporting connected with the modern version appears many decades after the supposed event, through later folklore writing.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufologie.patrickgross.org/ce3/1909-uk-avoch.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[Ufologie]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOS at close sight: URECAT-001496 - 1909, Avoch, Ross and Cromarty, U-K., twin boysApril 1, 2013...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 1, 2013</span></span></span>

This delay matters because memories and local traditions can change over time. A story passed through families, communities, or popular books may preserve a sense of place and cultural meaning while also gaining details that were not part of the original account. Features such as pointed hats, very small stature, and unusual clothing closely resemble longstanding European fairy and “little people” traditions rather than the language normally used in early UFO reports.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufologie.patrickgross.org/ce3/1909-uk-avoch.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[Ufologie]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOS at close sight: URECAT-001496 - 1909, Avoch, Ross and Cromarty, U-K., twin boysApril 1, 2013...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 1, 2013</span></span></span>

Avoch itself has a documented history of local customs and beliefs. Local heritage material records traditional superstitions and practices connected with names, animals, weddings, and ritual places, showing that folklore formed part of the area’s cultural background.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://avoch.org/old-avoch/superstitions" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: avoch.org">[avoch.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">avoch.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Superstitions – Avoch&#x27;s heritage</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Superstitions – Avoch&#x27;s heritage</span></span></span> This does not explain the Avoch story by itself, but it provides useful context for understanding why supernatural or unusual tales could exist alongside ordinary community history.

## Folklore versus documented investigations

The Avoch case highlights an important distinction in UFO research: not every unusual story is a UFO case in the evidential sense. A modern UFO investigation normally looks for details such as a contemporary witness statement, independent witnesses, physical evidence, photographs, radar information, official records, or a clear timeline. The Avoch account lacks these stronger forms of documentation.

The main evidence limits are:

* **No confirmed contemporary record:** there is no widely recognised 1909 investigation file or official report documenting the encounter.
* **Long reporting delay:** the story appears in later folklore and paranormal literature rather than through a direct surviving account from the alleged witnesses.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufologie.patrickgross.org/ce3/1909-uk-avoch.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[Ufologie]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOS at close sight: URECAT-001496 - 1909, Avoch, Ross and Cromarty, U-K., twin boysApril 1, 2013...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 1, 2013</span></span></span>
* **Folkloric imagery:** the description of small bearded figures and pointed hats fits older fairy traditions as easily as any modern unexplained-encounter interpretation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufologie.patrickgross.org/ce3/1909-uk-avoch.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologie.patrickgross.org">[Ufologie]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologie.patrickgross.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOS at close sight: URECAT-001496 - 1909, Avoch, Ross and Cromarty, U-K., twin boysApril 1, 2013...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 1, 2013</span></span></span>
* **Lack of independent confirmation:** there is no known physical evidence or separate witness record that verifies the event.

For this reason, the Avoch story is best understood as a folklore-linked mystery rather than a documented UFO encounter. It remains interesting because it shows how local legends can later be absorbed into wider discussions about unexplained phenomena.

    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
