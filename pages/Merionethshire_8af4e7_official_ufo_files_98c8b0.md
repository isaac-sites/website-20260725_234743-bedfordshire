## Introduction

The official record of UFO investigations in [Merionethshire]({{ 'what-really-happened-in-merionethshire/' | relative_url }}) is less a story of confirmed unexplained objects and more a record of how government bodies handled unusual reports. The county’s most famous case, the Berwyn Mountains incident of 23 January 1974, became internationally known because of claims of a possible crash and military response, but Ministry of Defence (MOD) files and later archival reviews show a more cautious picture: officials recorded reports, considered possible explanations and found no evidence of an extraterrestrial craft or recovered object.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/help-with-your-research/research-guides/ufos/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nationalarchives.gov.uk">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nationalarchives.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National Archives UFOs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesUFOs - The National Archives...</span></span></span>

For readers asking what the UK UFO files actually show, the answer is that they reveal a paper trail of sightings, correspondence, assessments and public enquiries. They show that unusual events were taken seriously enough to be logged, but they do not demonstrate that UFO reports represented alien technology. In Merionethshire, the records are most valuable because they show the gap between an event as experienced by witnesses and the conclusions reached after official assessment.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/explore-the-collection/explore-by-time-period/postwar/ufo-reports/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nationalarchives.gov.uk">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nationalarchives.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National Archives UFO reports</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesUFO reports - The National Archives...</span></span></span>

## Ministry of Defence UFO records

### What the files contain and what they do not

The MOD began retaining significant numbers of UFO-related records from the post-war period, particularly after public interest increased. The surviving files now held by The National Archives include sighting reports, correspondence with members of the public, internal policy discussions and responses to parliamentary or media enquiries. Earlier records were less complete because, before 1967, some UFO material was routinely destroyed after five years under MOD retention practices.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/help-with-your-research/research-guides/ufos/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nationalarchives.gov.uk">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nationalarchives.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National Archives UFOs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesUFOs - The National Archives...</span></span></span>

These documents were not a catalogue of proven UFO events. Most reports consisted of descriptions of lights, shapes or unusual movements, followed by attempts to identify ordinary explanations such as aircraft, satellites, planets, balloons or weather effects. The National Archives notes that many recorded sightings could often be explained, while others remained unusual because the available information was incomplete.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/explore-the-collection/explore-by-time-period/postwar/ufo-reports/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nationalarchives.gov.uk">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nationalarchives.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National Archives UFO reports</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesUFO reports - The National Archives...</span></span></span>

For Merionethshire, this distinction matters. The official interest in the Berwyn Mountains incident was not an endorsement of a “Welsh Roswell” interpretation. Instead, the files show the type of administrative response expected after reports of unusual lights, sounds and possible aviation incidents: collecting information, checking whether there was a defence concern and assessing whether further action was needed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/help-with-your-research/research-guides/ufos/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nationalarchives.gov.uk">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nationalarchives.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National Archives UFOs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesUFOs - The National Archives...</span></span></span>

### The Berwyn Mountains case in official records

The Berwyn Mountains incident became one of Wales’s best-known UFO stories because several elements appeared to overlap: reports of a bright flash, a loud noise, ground movement and speculation about an object coming down in the hills. Later retellings added claims of military recovery operations and secrecy, but those stronger claims are not supported by surviving official evidence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/help-with-your-research/research-guides/ufos/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nationalarchives.gov.uk">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nationalarchives.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National Archives UFOs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesUFOs - The National Archives...</span></span></span>

The official record is more limited. It documents an unusual event that attracted attention rather than a confirmed crash investigation. Later explanations focused on natural causes, particularly the combination of a meteor sighting and a small earthquake near the Bala area, which could account for reports of a flash, sound and shaking without requiring an unknown aircraft or spacecraft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/help-with-your-research/research-guides/ufos/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nationalarchives.gov.uk">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nationalarchives.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National Archives UFOs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesUFOs - The National Archives...</span></span></span>

The case demonstrates one of the main challenges in UFO record research: the original evidence is often a collection of separate observations rather than a single event captured by instruments. A bright light seen by one person, a sound heard by another and a tremor felt elsewhere can later become combined into one dramatic narrative, even when the official assessment treats them as separate phenomena.

## Archive searches and place names

### Finding Merionethshire records

Searching historic UFO records requires care because place names and administrative boundaries have changed. Merionethshire was a historic county of north-west Wales, while later local government changes altered the names and boundaries used in official records. A search for modern locations alone may miss older references, and a search for “Merionethshire” may not capture every report recorded under nearby towns, villages or former administrative descriptions.

The National Archives recommends searching UFO-related records through catalogue terms such as “UFO”, “unidentified flying”, and “saucers”, alongside location-based searches. Relevant material may appear in MOD files as well as records created by other departments or organisations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/help-with-your-research/research-guides/ufos/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nationalarchives.gov.uk">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nationalarchives.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National Archives UFOs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesUFOs - The National Archives...</span></span></span>

For local research, the most useful approach is usually to combine:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* historic county names such as Merionethshire;
* specific places including Bala, Llandrillo and the Berwyn Mountains;
* dates linked to known incidents;
* related terms such as aircraft, lights, explosions or unusual sightings.

</div>

This approach helps separate genuine archival references from later folklore. A newspaper report repeating a rumour is not the same type of evidence as an official sighting form, an internal assessment or a contemporaneous investigation note.

### Why archive material changed the public story

The release of MOD files through The National Archives changed UFO research in the UK because many previously discussed incidents could be examined through original documents rather than only through later books or television programmes. The files often showed a more ordinary administrative process: reports arrived, officials checked available information and many cases were closed without a finding of defence significance.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/explore-the-collection/explore-by-time-period/postwar/ufo-reports/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nationalarchives.gov.uk">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nationalarchives.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National Archives UFO reports</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesUFO reports - The National Archives...</span></span></span>

This did not eliminate every unanswered question. Some reports remained unresolved because witnesses provided limited information, no physical evidence existed or no independent measurements were available. However, “unresolved” in an archive does not mean “confirmed extraordinary”; it usually means the evidence was insufficient to identify the cause.

## How officials assessed reports

### Defence interest was the main question

The MOD’s role was not to prove or disprove extraterrestrial explanations. The practical concern was whether a reported object represented a threat to UK airspace, military security or aviation safety. Official assessments therefore focused on questions such as whether an aircraft, satellite, weather phenomenon or other known cause could explain a report.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/explore-the-collection/explore-by-time-period/postwar/ufo-reports/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nationalarchives.gov.uk">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nationalarchives.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National Archives UFO reports</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesUFO reports - The National Archives...</span></span></span>

A witness may describe something as a “craft”, while investigators may record only that an unusual light was observed and no evidence of a threat was found. The two accounts are not necessarily contradictory; they reflect different purposes.

### The end of routine MOD UFO investigations

The UK government continued publishing batches of UFO reports covering later decades, including reports from 1997 to 2009. These releases provide dates, locations and brief descriptions of sightings, allowing researchers to examine patterns without relying only on second-hand accounts.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ufo-reports-in-the-uk" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: GOV.UK">[GOV.UK]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">GOV.UK</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UF O reports in the UK</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO reports in the UK - GOV.UKDecember 4, 2007...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 4, 2007</span></span></span>

In 2009, the MOD ended its dedicated UFO investigation role, concluding that reviewing individual sightings did not provide useful defence value. The decision reflected the department’s assessment that no reported sighting had demonstrated a threat to the United Kingdom.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ufo-reports-in-the-uk" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: GOV.UK">[GOV.UK]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">GOV.UK</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UF O reports in the UK</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO reports in the UK - GOV.UKDecember 4, 2007...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 4, 2007</span></span></span>

For Merionethshire, this means that official records are best understood as evidence of reporting and assessment rather than evidence of hidden confirmation. The files preserve how authorities responded to unusual claims, but they do not support the idea that a government investigation verified an extraterrestrial event.

## What the files show about Merionethshire’s UFO history

Official records add an important layer to the county’s UFO history because they separate the original event from decades of later storytelling. The Berwyn Mountains incident remains significant because it shows how witness reports, natural phenomena, media coverage and public speculation can interact. The archival record confirms that the event attracted attention; it does not confirm the more dramatic interpretations that developed afterwards.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/help-with-your-research/research-guides/ufos/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nationalarchives.gov.uk">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nationalarchives.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National Archives UFOs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesUFOs - The National Archives...</span></span></span>

The wider lesson from Merionethshire’s case is that UFO archives are most useful when treated as evidence about investigations themselves. They reveal what was reported, what officials checked, what explanations were considered and where the evidence stopped.     if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
